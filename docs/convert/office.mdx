---
id: office
title: Convert office documents
---

Turn Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files, and over a hundred other formats, into PDF with LibreOffice. Works on `/forms/libreoffice/convert`.

## Convert a document

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

The PDF takes the document's name, `report.docx.pdf`, unless the `Gotenberg-Output-Filename` header names it. The [LibreOffice reference](/docs/reference/libreoffice#formats) lists every format.

## Convert several

Upload several documents to get one PDF each, in a ZIP:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx", "annex.docx"]}
  output="documents.zip"
/>

## Merge them

Add a `merge` stage for one PDF instead:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx", "annex.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "merge" }]}
  output="documents.pdf"
/>

The documents merge in the alphanumeric order of their names, so `annex.docx` comes first. Name them `1-report.docx` and `2-annex.docx` to pick the order.

## Open a protected document

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["protected.docx"]}
  fields={{ password: "secret" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Without the password, or with a wrong one, Gotenberg answers 400 and says which.

## Fit a spreadsheet on one page

A spreadsheet spreads over as many pages as its print settings ask. `sales.csv` gives three. `singlePage` puts each sheet on one page:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["sales.csv"]}
  fields={{ singlePage: true }}
  output="sales.pdf"
/>

## Make it archival

LibreOffice writes PDF/A itself with `nativePdfa`:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  fields={{ nativePdfa: "PDF/A-2b" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

The `remediate` stage does the same after the conversion, on any route and for every PDF/A level, and reports what it couldn't fix:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "remediate", profile: "PDF/A-2b" }]}
  output="report.pdf"
  response={`HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: application/pdf
Gotenberg-Remediation-Residual: 0
Gotenberg-Remediation-Rolled-Back: 0`}
/>

Both PDFs pass Gotenberg's own PDF/A-2b [validation](/docs/reference/pdf#validate). Nothing checks `nativePdfa`'s output, though, and a stage after it, such as `stamp-text`, can break its conformance. `remediate` comes after the other stages and reports what's left.
