---
id: encrypt
title: Encrypt and decrypt PDFs
---

Protect a PDF with passwords, restrict what readers may do with it, and lift the protection again.

## Require a password

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/encrypt"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ userPassword: "secret" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

A viewer asks for `secret` before it opens the PDF.

## Restrict what readers may do

The owner password guards the permissions: printing, copying text, editing, filling forms, assembling pages. Turn off the ones you don't grant:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/encrypt"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{
    userPassword: "secret",
    ownerPassword: "owner",
    permissions: { print: false, extractText: false },
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`permissions` takes `print`, `printHighQuality`, `modifyContent`, `extractText`, `modifyAnnotations`, `fillForms` and `assemble`, each `true` unless you set it to `false`. Viewers enforce them; a tool that ignores them can still print. Only the user password keeps the content itself secret.

## Pick the algorithm

`algorithm` takes `aes256`, the default, `aes128`, `rc4-128` or `rc4-40`. Keep AES-256 unless an old reader can't open it.

## Encrypt while converting

Encryption comes last in a list of stages: only `optimize` and `render` may follow it.

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "encrypt", userPassword: "secret" }]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

A PDF/A or Factur-X can't be encrypted, and an encrypted PDF can't be signed: the [order rules](/docs/reference/stages#order-rules) answer 400 first.

## Decrypt a PDF

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/decrypt"
  files={["encrypted.pdf"]}
  fields={{ password: "secret" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

In a list of stages, `decrypt` comes first, and the stages after it work on the open PDF. The [read routes](/docs/reference/pdf#read-pdfs) take a `password` field instead.
