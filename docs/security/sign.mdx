---
id: sign
title: Sign and timestamp PDFs
---

Sign a PDF with your certificate, so readers can check who signed it and that nothing changed since, and prove when it existed.

## Sign a PDF

Upload the PDF, a PEM certificate and its private key:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/sign"
  files={["report.pdf", "cert.pem", "key.pem"]}
  fields={{
    certificate: "cert.pem",
    key: "key.pem",
    reason: "Approved",
    location: "Paris",
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`reason`, `location`, `contactInfo` and `name` show in the viewer's signature panel. `cert.pem` is a self-signed test certificate, so viewers flag the signature as untrusted: sign with a certificate from an authority they trust.

## Sign with a PKCS#12 file

A `.p12` or `.pfx` file holds the certificate and its key. `certificatePassword` opens it:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/sign"
  files={["report.pdf", "cert.p12"]}
  fields={{ certificate: "cert.p12", certificatePassword: "secret" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Make it PAdES

`pades: true` makes a PAdES signature, the format the EU's eIDAS regulation recognizes:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/sign"
  files={["report.pdf", "cert.pem", "key.pem"]}
  fields={{ certificate: "cert.pem", key: "key.pem", pades: true }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Timestamp the signature

`timestampUrl` asks an RFC 3161 timestamp authority to vouch for the signing time:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/sign"
  files={["report.pdf", "cert.pem", "key.pem"]}
  fields={{
    certificate: "cert.pem",
    key: "key.pem",
    pades: true,
    timestampUrl: "http://timestamp.digicert.com",
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Timestamp without signing

A document timestamp proves the PDF existed, unchanged, at that time, without naming a signer:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/timestamp"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ timestampUrl: "http://timestamp.digicert.com" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Operators decide which authorities Gotenberg may call with [`--pdf-allow-list`](/docs/configuration#pdf-allow-list), [`--pdf-deny-list`](/docs/configuration#pdf-deny-list) and [`--pdf-deny-private-ips`](/docs/configuration#pdf-deny-private-ips).

## Sign last

A change after a signature breaks it, so only another `sign`, a `timestamp` or a `render` may follow a `sign` stage. Optimize before you sign: `optimize` refuses a signed PDF, since its rewrite would drop the signature.

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf", "cert.pem", "key.pem"]}
  stages={[
    { op: "optimize" },
    { op: "sign", certificate: "cert.pem", key: "key.pem", pades: true },
  ]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>
