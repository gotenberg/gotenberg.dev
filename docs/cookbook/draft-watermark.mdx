title: Mark Word documents as drafts
---

You have a Word document under review. You want its PDF to say DRAFT across every page, without editing the document.

## Request

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[
    {
      op: "stamp-text",
      text: "DRAFT",
      x: 200,
      y: 290,
      fontSize: 96,
      color: "#b42318",
      opacity: 0.2,
      rotation: 45,
    },
  ]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Result

A Letter PDF with a faint red DRAFT across the middle of each page, over the content.

## How it works

- LibreOffice converts the document, then `stamp-text` draws on every page: see [Stamp and watermark PDFs](/docs/process/stamps).
- `x` and `y` count in points from the bottom-left corner, and `rotation` turns the text around that point. On a Letter page, 612 by 792 points, these values center it.
- `opacity` keeps the content readable under it.

## Variations

- Some pages only: add `pages`, such as `"1"`.
- A logo instead of text: [`stamp-image`](/docs/process/stamps#stamp-an-image).
- LibreOffice's own watermark: [`nativeWatermarkText`](/docs/reference/libreoffice#nativeWatermarkText) sizes the text to the page.
- PDFs you already have: send them to `/forms/pdf/stamp-text` with the same options.
