title: Thumbnails of any document
---

You have uploads of all kinds: PDFs, Word documents, web pages. You want a small preview image of each one's first page.

## Request

For a PDF, render its first page:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/render"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1", dpi: 48 }}
  output="report_1.png"
/>

## Result

`report_1.png`, 298 by 421 pixels: the page at two-thirds of its size in points.

## How it works

- `render` draws the pages you pick, and the route answers the image alone: see [Render pages as images](/docs/pdf/render).
- `dpi` sets the size: 72 gives a pixel per point, 48 two-thirds of that.

## Variations

- A Word document: convert it and render in the same request. The answer is a ZIP with the PDF and `report.docx_1.png`:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "render", pages: "1", dpi: 48 }]}
  output="report.zip"
/>

- A web page: [take a screenshot](/docs/chromium/screenshot-url) with `clip`, `width` and `height` for a fixed size.
- JPEG, for smaller files: add `format: jpeg` and a `quality`.
