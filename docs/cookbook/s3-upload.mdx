---
id: s3-upload
title: Upload results to S3
---

You have documents to convert, and an S3 bucket, or any store that speaks its API. You want the PDFs in the bucket, without waiting for them.

## Request

Have your backend create a presigned `PUT` URL for the object, then hand it to Gotenberg:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  headers={{
    "Gotenberg-Webhook-Url":
      "https://my-bucket.s3.amazonaws.com/report.pdf?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Signature=…",
    "Gotenberg-Webhook-Method": "PUT",
    "Gotenberg-Webhook-Events-Url": "https://api.example.com/gotenberg-events",
  }}
  status={204}
  run={false}
/>

## Result

Gotenberg answers `204 No Content` at once. When the PDF is ready, it uploads it to the presigned URL, and posts a `webhook.success` event to your events URL, or `webhook.error` with the reason.

## How it works

- `Gotenberg-Webhook-Method: PUT` sends the PDF as the request body, which is what a presigned `PUT` expects. Gotenberg keeps the URL's query string, signature included.
- The events URL is required: it tells your system the object is there, or why it isn't. See [Deliver with webhooks](/docs/webhook-download).
- The presigned URL keeps your credentials out of Gotenberg: it can write that one object, until it expires.

## Variations

- A URL that signs a `Content-Type`: sign `application/pdf`, the type Gotenberg sends with one PDF, or `application/zip` for several files.
- Your events endpoint checks a secret: send it with `Gotenberg-Webhook-Extra-Http-Headers`, which reaches the upload too.
- A private bucket endpoint, or an internal store such as MinIO: allow its host with [`--webhook-allow-list`](/docs/operate/outbound#match-urls-with-lists) when the webhook filters are on.
