Where to ask, and what to send so someone can answer.

## Search first

[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) covers the common symptoms. Gotenberg's [GitHub issues](https://github.com/gotenberg/gotenberg/issues) and [discussions](https://github.com/gotenberg/gotenberg/discussions) hold the rest.

## Ask a question

Ask on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/gotenberg/gotenberg/discussions): how to do something, whether Gotenberg fits a use case, how to deploy it.

## Report a bug

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/gotenberg/gotenberg/issues) with what it takes to reproduce it:

- The version: `GET /version`, or the `Gotenberg-Version` header of any answer, and the image variant.
- The request, as a cURL command with the files, cut down to the smallest one that still fails.
- The answer: its status, its message and its `Gotenberg-Trace`.
- Gotenberg's logs for that trace, at [`--log-level=debug`](/docs/configuration#log-level) if you can.
- The configuration: the answer of [`GET /debug`](/docs/system/debug), with `--api-enable-debug-route` on. Remove anything private first.
