LibreOffice converts office documents to PDF: Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, OpenDocument files, and over a hundred other formats. It answers on one route, `POST /forms/libreoffice/convert`.

## Convert documents

Upload the documents under `files`:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Each document becomes a PDF named after it: `report.docx` gives `report.docx.pdf`. Several documents answer a ZIP of PDFs:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx", "annex.docx"]}
  output="documents.zip"
/>

Add a `merge` stage for one PDF instead. [Stages](/docs/reference/stages) run on the converted PDFs, in the alphanumeric order of the documents' names:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx", "annex.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "merge" }]}
  output="documents.pdf"
/>

## Fields

A field whose name starts with `native` asks LibreOffice itself. A stage does the same job on the PDF afterwards, the same way for every route: `nativeWatermarkText` has `stamp-text`, `nativePdfa` has `remediate`.
