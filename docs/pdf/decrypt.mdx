Opens encrypted PDFs and removes their encryption.

<Endpoint op="decrypt" />

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/decrypt"
  files={["encrypted.pdf"]}
  fields={{ password: "secret" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

In a list of stages, `decrypt` comes first, and the stages after it work on the open PDF. It doesn't combine with `sign` or `timestamp`: sign the decrypted PDF in a request of its own. The [read routes](/docs/pdf/info#read-an-encrypted-pdf) take a `password` field instead.
