Sets the rotation of pages: a quarter, a half or three quarters of a turn.

<Endpoint op="rotate" />

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/rotate"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ angle: 90, pages: "1" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Viewers turn the page by the angle, clockwise. `-90` turns it the other way. The angle replaces the rotation the page has, so `90` on a page already at `90` changes nothing, and `0` straightens it. Without `pages`, every page takes the angle.
