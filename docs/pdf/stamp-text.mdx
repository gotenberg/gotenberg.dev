Draws a line of text over pages: a watermark, a label, a status.

<Endpoint op="stamp-text" />

<Place />

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/stamp-text"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{
    text: "CONFIDENTIAL",
    x: 120,
    y: 250,
    fontSize: 36,
    color: "#b42318",
    opacity: 0.4,
    rotation: 30,
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`rotation` turns the text counter-clockwise around `x` and `y`. `font` takes one of the 14 standard PDF fonts, `Helvetica` by default, which take Western European text only; `Symbol` and `ZapfDingbats` draw it as symbols. For other scripts, stamp a page Chromium renders with [`stamp-pdf`](/docs/pdf/stamp-pdf), or use LibreOffice's [`nativeWatermarkText`](/docs/libreoffice/convert#nativeWatermarkText).

A stamp only covers what's under it: [redact](/docs/pdf/redact) to remove content for good.
