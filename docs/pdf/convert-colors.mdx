Converts every color of a PDF to one color space.

<Endpoint op="convert-colors" />

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/convert-colors"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ target: "gray" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`target` takes `cmyk` for a print shop, `gray` for black and white printing, `rgb` for screens, or `output-intent` for the one the PDF declares.
