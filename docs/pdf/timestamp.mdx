Adds a document timestamp from an RFC 3161 timestamp authority: proof the PDF existed, unchanged, at that time.

<Endpoint op="timestamp" />

## Timestamp without signing

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/timestamp"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ timestampUrl: "http://timestamp.digicert.com" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

A document timestamp names no signer. To timestamp a signature, give [`sign`](/docs/pdf/sign#timestamp-the-signature) a `timestampUrl` instead.

## Pick the authorities

Operators decide which authorities Gotenberg may call with [`--pdf-allow-list`](/docs/configuration#pdf-allow-list), [`--pdf-deny-list`](/docs/configuration#pdf-deny-list) and [`--pdf-deny-private-ips`](/docs/configuration#pdf-deny-private-ips): see [Restrict outbound requests](/docs/operate/outbound).
