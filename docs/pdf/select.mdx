Keeps the pages you list, in the order you list them.

<Endpoint op="select" />

## Keep pages

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1-2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`pages` takes a [page selection](/docs/pdf/pipe#page-selections): pages, ranges such as `2-5` or `3-`, `odd`, `even` or `last`, and exclusions such as `!1`. `all`, on its own, keeps every page.

## Reorder pages

`select` keeps the pages in the order you list them:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "3,1,2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Exclude pages

A `!` drops the pages of the part it precedes. `!1` keeps every page but the first:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "!1" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>
