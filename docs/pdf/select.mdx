Keeps the pages you list, in the order you list them.

<Endpoint op="select" />

## Keep pages

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1-2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`pages` takes a [page selection](/docs/pdf/pipe#page-selections): a page, a range, a list of both, or `all`, `odd`, `even` or `last`.

## Reorder pages

`select` keeps the pages in the order you list them:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "3,1,2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>
