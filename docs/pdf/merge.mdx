Combines PDFs into one.

<Endpoint op="merge" />

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/merge"
  files={["report.pdf", "annex.pdf"]}
  output="merged.pdf"
/>

Gotenberg merges the files in the alphanumeric order of their names, not in the order you upload them: `annex.pdf` comes first here. Name them `1-report.pdf` and `2-annex.pdf` to pick the order. The result takes the name of the first file.

Each PDF keeps its bookmarks, pointed at the merged pages. For one bookmark per file, add a [`set-bookmarks`](/docs/pdf/set-bookmarks) stage after `merge`.

<AsStage op="merge" />

Conversions merge too: a `merge` stage turns [several office documents](/docs/libreoffice/convert#merge-them) into one PDF.
