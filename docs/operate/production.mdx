Size, tune and scale Gotenberg for real traffic.

## What one container does

A container runs one Chromium and one LibreOffice, both started on first use:

- Chromium converts up to six pages at once: [`--chromium-max-concurrency`](/docs/configuration#chromium-max-concurrency).
- LibreOffice converts one document at a time.
- The PDF stages process a request's documents one after the other. [`--pdf-max-concurrency`](/docs/configuration#pdf-max-concurrency) lets a request with several documents process up to that many at once, for more memory and CPU. Requests themselves never wait for one another here.

The other requests wait in a queue. To serve more at once, run more containers behind a load balancer: Gotenberg keeps no state between requests.

## Bound the waits

- [`--api-timeout`](/docs/configuration#api-timeout) bounds each request, 30 seconds by default, queue included. A request past it answers 503.
- [`--chromium-max-queue-size`](/docs/configuration#chromium-max-queue-size) and [`--libreoffice-max-queue-size`](/docs/configuration#libreoffice-max-queue-size) cap the queues, unlimited by default. A request past the cap answers 429: retry it, or route it to another container.

## Start fast

The first request starts the engine it needs, and pays for it. Start them with the container instead:

```yaml title="compose.yaml"
services:
  gotenberg:
    image: gotenberg/gotenberg:9
    environment:
      CHROMIUM_AUTO_START: "true"
      LIBREOFFICE_AUTO_START: "true"
```

[`--chromium-idle-shutdown-timeout`](/docs/configuration#chromium-idle-shutdown-timeout) and [`--libreoffice-idle-shutdown-timeout`](/docs/configuration#libreoffice-idle-shutdown-timeout) do the opposite: they stop an idle engine to free its memory.

## Restart the engines

Chromium restarts every 100 conversions and LibreOffice every 10, which keeps their memory in check. [`--chromium-restart-after`](/docs/configuration#chromium-restart-after) and [`--libreoffice-restart-after`](/docs/configuration#libreoffice-restart-after) change the counts; `0` turns the restarts off.

## Probe the health

`/health` answers 200 while Gotenberg and its engines are up, and 503 when one is down. It never asks for credentials. Point your liveness and readiness probes at it: see [Kubernetes](/docs/getting-started/installation#kubernetes) and [the health route](/docs/reference/system#health).

## Shut down gracefully

On `SIGTERM` or `SIGINT`, Gotenberg stops taking requests and waits for the running ones, webhooks included, up to [`--gotenberg-graceful-shutdown-duration`](/docs/configuration#gotenberg-graceful-shutdown-duration), 30 seconds by default. Give your orchestrator a grace period at least as long.
