Merge, split, stamp, redact, fill, sign and convert PDFs: a PDF you upload, or one Gotenberg just made.

Each operation is a stage, and works three ways:

- On a PDF you upload, with the operation's own route: `/forms/pdf/merge`, `/forms/pdf/stamp-text`, and so on. Its options are form fields.
- On a PDF you upload, as part of a list, with `/forms/pdf/pipe` and its `stages` field.
- On a PDF Gotenberg makes, with the `stages` field of a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion.

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "stamp-text", text: "DRAFT", x: 200, y: 290 }]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

[How requests work](/docs/getting-started/requests#add-stages) explains the `stages` field, and [Stages](/docs/reference/stages) lists every stage and its options.
