---
id: pages
title: Organize pages
---

Keep, reorder, remove, insert and rotate the pages of a PDF.

## Keep pages

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1-2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`pages` takes a [page selection](/docs/reference/stages#page-selections): a page, a range, a list of both, or `odd`, `even` or `last`.

## Reorder pages

`select` keeps the pages in the order you list them:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/select"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "3,1,2" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Remove pages

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/delete"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "last" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Insert pages

Insert the pages of another PDF after a given page:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/insert"
  files={["report.pdf", "annex.pdf"]}
  fields={{ source: "annex.pdf", at: 1 }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`at: 0` inserts before the first page, and `sourcePages` picks the pages of `annex.pdf` to insert. The stage reads `annex.pdf`, so Gotenberg doesn't process it as a PDF of its own.

## Rotate pages

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/rotate"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ angle: 90, pages: "1" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Viewers turn the page by the angle, clockwise. `-90` turns it the other way.

## Chain them

A pipe runs several of these in one request:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  stages={[
    { op: "select", pages: "1-2" },
    { op: "rotate", angle: 90, pages: "2" },
  ]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

The page numbers of each stage count from the PDF the previous one produced.
