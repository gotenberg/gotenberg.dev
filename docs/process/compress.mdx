---
id: compress
title: Compress PDFs
---

Make PDFs smaller, and fit them to printers: fewer bytes, no transparency, one color space. Works on any PDF: a PDF you upload, through the stage's own route or `/forms/pdf/pipe`, and a PDF a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion makes, through its `stages`.

## Optimize a PDF

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/optimize"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Gotenberg rewrites the whole file. It packs objects into compressed streams, stores identical streams once, recompresses images without losing quality, and keeps only the glyphs the document shows. This report goes from 23 to 20 KB. A page with large images shrinks more: a Chromium page with a few loses a third.

## Optimize what you make

Put `optimize` after the stages that change the content, such as a merge:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf", "annex.pdf"]}
  stages={[{ op: "merge" }, { op: "optimize" }]}
  output="documents.pdf"
/>

A conversion takes it too:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "optimize" }]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Turn a pass off

Each pass runs unless you turn it off: `objectStreams`, `dedup`, `recompressImages` and `subsetFonts`. Recompressing large images takes time, for instance:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/optimize"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ recompressImages: false }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Signed and encrypted PDFs

A rewrite removes signatures, so a signed PDF answers 400: optimize first, then sign. A rewrite also writes an encrypted PDF decrypted, so an encrypted PDF answers 400 unless the stages decrypt or encrypt it as well:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["encrypted.pdf"]}
  stages={[{ op: "decrypt", password: "secret" }, { op: "optimize" }]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

A PDF/A-1 stays conformant: Gotenberg leaves out the object streams PDF/A-1 forbids.

## Flatten transparency

Some printers and archival formats don't take transparency. `flatten-transparency` replaces it with opaque content:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/flatten-transparency"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Convert the colors

`convert-colors` puts every color in one color space: `cmyk` for a print shop, `gray` for black and white printing, `rgb` for screens, or `output-intent` for the one the PDF declares:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/convert-colors"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ target: "gray" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

## Lower the image quality

`optimize` never lowers the quality of an image. To trade quality for size, set it where the PDF is made: LibreOffice's [`nativeQuality` and `nativeReduceImageResolution`](/docs/reference/libreoffice#images), or smaller images in the HTML you send Chromium.
