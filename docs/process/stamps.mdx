---
id: stamps
title: Stamp and watermark PDFs
---

Draw text, an image or a page of another PDF over the pages: a watermark, a logo, a letterhead. Works on any PDF: a PDF you upload, through the stage's own route or `/forms/pdf/pipe`, and a PDF a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion makes, through its `stages`.

## Place a stamp

A stamp's `x` and `y` count in points from the bottom-left corner of the page, 72 points to the inch. A Letter page measures 612 by 792 points, an A4 page 595 by 842. Without `pages`, a stamp goes on every page.

## Stamp text

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/stamp-text"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{
    text: "CONFIDENTIAL",
    x: 120,
    y: 250,
    fontSize: 36,
    color: "#b42318",
    opacity: 0.4,
    rotation: 30,
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

`rotation` turns the text counter-clockwise around `x` and `y`. `font` takes one of the 14 standard PDF fonts, `Helvetica` by default.

## Stamp an image

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/stamp-image"
  files={["report.pdf", "logo.png"]}
  fields={{ image: "logo.png", x: 374, y: 24, width: 48, pages: "1" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Set `width` or `height` and Gotenberg keeps the image's proportions. The image is a PNG or a JPEG.

## Stamp a letterhead

`stamp-pdf` draws a page of another PDF over each page:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/stamp-pdf"
  files={["report.pdf", "letterhead.pdf"]}
  fields={{ source: "letterhead.pdf" }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Draw the letterhead on a transparent page, so the content under it shows: Chromium makes one from HTML with `printBackground` and `omitBackground`. `scale` resizes the page, and `sourcePage` picks another page of the source.

## Stamp while converting

Stamps are stages, so a conversion takes them too:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[
    {
      op: "stamp-text",
      text: "DRAFT",
      x: 200,
      y: 400,
      fontSize: 48,
      opacity: 0.3,
      rotation: 45,
    },
  ]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>
