---
id: render
title: Render pages as images
---

Draw the pages of a PDF as PNG or JPEG images: thumbnails, previews, social cards. Works on any PDF: a PDF you upload, through the stage's own route or `/forms/pdf/pipe`, and a PDF a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion makes, through its `stages`.

## Render a page

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/render"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1" }}
  output="report_1.png"
/>

One image answers on its own, named after the PDF and the page: `report_1.png`. Several come back in a ZIP.

## Pick the resolution and the format

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/render"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ pages: "1", dpi: 72, format: "jpeg", quality: 80 }}
  output="report_1.jpeg"
/>

`dpi` sets the resolution, 150 by default. At 72, a pixel stands for a point, so a Letter page gives a 612 by 792 image. `quality` applies to JPEG.

## Render every page

Without `pages`, Gotenberg draws them all:

<Request route="/forms/pdf/render" files={["report.pdf"]} output="report.zip" />

The ZIP holds `report_1.png`, `report_2.png` and `report_3.png`.

## Render after other stages

`render` comes last in a list of stages. The ZIP then holds the PDF and its images:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  stages={[
    { op: "stamp-text", text: "DRAFT", x: 72, y: 72, fontSize: 24 },
    { op: "render", pages: "1" },
  ]}
  output="report.zip"
/>

Fonts the PDF doesn't embed, such as the stamp's Helvetica, draw with the fonts in Gotenberg's image.

## Preview a document

A conversion renders too. This one answers the PDF of a Word document and a thumbnail of its first page:

<Request
  route="/forms/libreoffice/convert"
  files={["report.docx"]}
  stages={[{ op: "render", pages: "1", dpi: 72 }]}
  output="report.zip"
/>

The ZIP holds `report.docx.pdf` and `report.docx_1.png`.
