---
id: redact
title: Redact content
---

Remove text and images from a PDF for good, not just hide them. Works on any PDF: a PDF you upload, through the stage's own route or `/forms/pdf/pipe`, and a PDF a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion makes, through its `stages`.

## Redact a region

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/redact"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{
    regions: [{ page: 1, x: 160, y: 435, width: 60, height: 25 }],
  }}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Gotenberg removes the content under each region from the file itself: the "Page 1" label is gone from the page and from any text a reader or a script extracts. Nothing is drawn in its place.

## Measure the region

A region's `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` count in points from the bottom-left corner of the page, 72 points to the inch. The pages of this report measure 446 by 631 points, and its label sits about 440 points up from the bottom.

## Mark the redaction

Stamp a black box over the region to show where content was. A one-pixel black PNG stretches to the size you give it:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf", "black.png"]}
  stages={[
    {
      op: "redact",
      regions: [{ page: 1, x: 160, y: 435, width: 60, height: 25 }],
    },
    {
      op: "stamp-image",
      image: "black.png",
      pages: "1",
      x: 160,
      y: 435,
      width: 60,
      height: 25,
    },
  ]}
  output="report.pdf"
/>

Redact first: a stamp only covers what's under it.
