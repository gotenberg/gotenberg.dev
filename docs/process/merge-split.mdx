---
id: merge-split
title: Merge and split PDFs
---

Combine PDFs into one, or cut one into several. Works on any PDF: a PDF you upload, through the stage's own route or `/forms/pdf/pipe`, and a PDF a Chromium or LibreOffice conversion makes, through its `stages`.

## Merge PDFs

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/merge"
  files={["report.pdf", "annex.pdf"]}
  output="merged.pdf"
/>

Gotenberg merges the files in the alphanumeric order of their names, not in the order you upload them: `annex.pdf` comes first here. Name them `1-report.pdf` and `2-annex.pdf` to pick the order. The result takes the name of the first file.

Conversions merge too: add a `merge` stage to [convert several office documents](/docs/libreoffice/convert#merge-them) into one PDF.

## Split every page

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/split"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ every: 1 }}
  output="report.zip"
/>

The answer is a ZIP of `report_0.pdf`, `report_1.pdf` and `report_2.pdf`. `every: 2` cuts every two pages instead.

## Split by ranges

`ranges` gives one PDF per entry, each a [page selection](/docs/reference/stages#page-selections):

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/split"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ ranges: ["1", "2-3"] }}
  output="report.zip"
/>

## Process each part

The stages after a split run on each part. This one stamps both parts:

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/pipe"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  stages={[
    { op: "split", ranges: ["1", "2-3"] },
    { op: "stamp-text", text: "DRAFT", x: 36, y: 36, fontSize: 18 },
  ]}
  output="report.zip"
/>
