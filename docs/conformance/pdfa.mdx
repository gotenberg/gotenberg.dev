Turn any PDF into an archival PDF/A, and check that it conforms.

## Remediate a PDF

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/remediate"
  files={["report.pdf"]}
  fields={{ profile: "PDF/A-2b" }}
  output="report.pdf"
  response={`HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: application/pdf
Gotenberg-Remediation-Residual: 0
Gotenberg-Remediation-Rolled-Back: 0`}
/>

The `remediate` stage repairs the PDF until it meets the profile. Two headers report how it went:

- `Gotenberg-Remediation-Residual` counts the violations left. `0` means the PDF conforms.
- `Gotenberg-Remediation-Rolled-Back` counts the fixes Gotenberg undid because they changed how the pages look. `aggressive: true` keeps them, for better compliance.

Gotenberg answers 200 even when violations remain: check `Gotenberg-Remediation-Residual`, and list what's left with [`/forms/pdf/validate`](#validate-a-pdf). With several PDFs, the headers sum their counts.

## Pick a level

`profile` takes `PDF/A-1a`, `PDF/A-1b`, `PDF/A-2a`, `PDF/A-2b`, `PDF/A-2u`, `PDF/A-3a`, `PDF/A-3b`, `PDF/A-3u`, `PDF/A-4`, `PDF/A-4e` or `PDF/A-4f`.

- The letter sets how much the PDF keeps: `b` its look, `u` its text as Unicode too, `a` its structure too, through tags.
- The number sets the base: PDF/A-1 builds on PDF 1.4, PDF/A-2 adds transparency and layers, PDF/A-3 accepts embedded files of any type, and PDF/A-4 builds on PDF 2.0.

Pick the level your archive or portal asks for. Without one, PDF/A-4 is the latest version. An e-invoice needs PDF/A-3: see [Create Factur-X invoices](/docs/conformance/factur-x).

## Convert to PDF/A

Add the stage to a conversion:

<Request
  route="/forms/chromium/convert/html"
  files={["index.html", "style.css"]}
  stages={[{ op: "remediate", profile: "PDF/A-2b" }]}
  output="invoice.pdf"
/>

LibreOffice also writes PDF/A itself, with [`nativePdfa`](/docs/convert/office#make-it-archival).

## Validate a PDF

<Request
  route="/forms/pdf/validate"
  files={["invoice.pdf"]}
  fields={{ profile: "PDF/A-3b" }}
  response={{
    "invoice.pdf": { profile: "PDF/A-3b", compliant: true, violations: [] },
  }}
/>

A PDF that doesn't conform lists its violations, each with the clause it breaks: see [the validate route](/docs/reference/pdf#validate).
